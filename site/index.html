Launch without a checkout

Keep it in your profile

nix profile install github:flakesonnix/wallbreak

Know your terrain.

Wallbreak is experimental, not a full Wallpaper Engine clone. Support depends on your compositor and the content you choose.

Read the compatibility details →
Sway & layer-shell
Images, videos, and supported scenes on Wayland compositors implementing wlr-layer-shell. Tested with Sway.
GNOME
Static images through gsettings. Video and supported scene playback require the optional GNOME 50 Shell bridge, installed and enabled separately.
Deliberately limited
No X11, web or application wallpapers, custom scene shaders, or full scene compatibility. Video decoding uses an external FFmpeg process on the CPU, not hardware decoding.
